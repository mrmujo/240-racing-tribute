240 Racing

A JavaScript re-implementation of the 1998 DOS game by Alexander Sandström, Mikael Sjöberg and Lars Andersson.

New tracks, cars, font and pictures made for this project. Nothing to download.

or play the original

    Red car: A accelerate, Z brake, X reverse, S/F steer. Yellow car: Ctrl accelerate, Alt brake, ↓ reverse, ←/→ steer. Space race, F1 help, F2 options (players, laps, track, keys), H hiscores, +/− sound, Esc quit. On macOS, Ctrl+arrows may switch desktops; remap the yellow car's keys under F2. Your game files stay in this browser and are never uploaded.
